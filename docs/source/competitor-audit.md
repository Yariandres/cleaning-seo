# Competitor Website Audit – Poznań

Nov 6, 2026 · @Yari

## Key takeaways

- **The bar is set by two sites.** Pranie Posejdona and OtoCzystaTapicerka win with the same formula: a WhatsApp photo quote in one tap, prices on the homepage and their review count front and centre. We need all three from day one.
- **Google's #1 spot is beatable.** PranieNaZawolanie24 ranks first with an old, text-heavy page that has no reviews, no WhatsApp and no prices on the homepage. A clear site with separate service pages can overtake it.
- **Nobody owns strollers, car seats or pets.** No Poznań competitor has a dedicated page for either; strollers are a line in a price list or tucked under mattresses.
- **Suburbs are treated as an extra cost.** Competitors either charge for travel outside Poznań or just list town names. Free travel in the near suburbs is a real differentiator.
- **Only one competitor offers a guarantee and insurance.** Posejdona does; the local firms don't. It's an easy trust win.
- **Watch stroller prices.** One local firm lists strollers and car seats at 60–120 zł, so our launch prices now sit in that range.

## Notes per competitor

### Pranie Posejdona – the benchmark

Headline: *"Mobilne pranie tapicerki, materacy, dywanów i wykładzin"*, with "5,0 w Google · ponad 500 opinii" right above it.

- **Works:** four promises under the headline (minimum order 250 zł, slot "nawet na jutro", travel included, "gwarancja poprawki"); green WhatsApp button for a photo quote "usually within minutes"; clear "od" prices (sofa from 160 zł, corner sofa from 270 zł, mattress from 200 zł); 3-step process; OC insurance; a 3-day re-clean guarantee; open daily 7–21; B2B section; equipment rental and chemical shop.
- **Weak:** a national chain, so nothing feels local to Poznań; drying "up to 36 h" unless you pay +50% for drying; 250 zł minimum shuts out small jobs; no strollers, car seats or family angle; car upholstery offered only in Olsztyn and Trójmiasto.

### OtoCzystaTapicerka – the best local brand

Headline: *"Oto czystość. Na nowo."*, with "Ulubiona kanapa. Rodzinne auto. Dobry sen." underneath.

- **Works:** warm, modern brand with a consistent "Oto…" voice; review count in the Google page title ("5,0 w Google"); "Wyślij zdjęcie na WhatsApp" as the main button; service pages for sofas, corner sofas and mattresses in Poznań; area page naming Swarzędz, Suchy Las, Luboń, Komorniki, Mosina, Rokietnica, Tarnowo Podgórne; stain first-aid guide; 10% referral discount; 10 years' experience.
- **Weak:** prices are net plus 23% VAT, which confuses consumers (sofa "od 150 zł netto" is really about 185 zł); strollers and car seats buried under mattresses; no guarantee or insurance; no quote form; drying answer hidden in a collapsed FAQ.

### Karcher Czyszczenie Poznań – SEO without warmth

Headline: *"Karcher Czyszczenie – pranie dywanów, wykładzin, tapicerek oraz materacy"*.

- **Works:** a page per service (carpets, mattresses, car, sofas); blog; three phone numbers for Poznań, Duszniki and Środa Wielkopolska.
- **Weak:** long generic text written for Google, not people; leads with machine model numbers (Puzzi 8/1); no WhatsApp, no form, no reviews on the page; no drying time, guarantee or FAQ.

### PranieNaZawolanie24 – ranks #1 with the weakest site

Headline: the company name and phone number; page title starts "Tanie pranie…".

- **Works:** an old, keyword-rich site that Google trusts; lists south-western suburbs by name (Komorniki, Plewiska, Luboń, Przeźmierowo, Skórzewo, Dopiewo, Mosina, Stęszew); issues invoices; positioned as the cheap option.
- **Weak:** walls of keyword-stuffed text; phone is the only contact; no prices on the homepage, no reviews, no photos of work, no FAQ. Proof that the top spot is beatable with a better site.

### Czysta Tapicerka Poznań – transparent but basic

Headline: *"Czysta Tapicerka – Poznań i okolice"*, "Doświadczenie od 2014 roku".

- **Works:** full price ranges on the homepage (sofa 150–250 zł, corner sofa 200–500 zł, mattress 120–170 zł); **strollers, carriers and car seats listed at 60–120 zł**; FAQ with job lengths; Saturday work; carpet packages; WhatsApp and a form.
- **Weak:** one long page, so it can't rank for separate services; generic promises; travel outside Poznań costs extra (suburbs feel second-class); no reviews or guarantee on the page.

## What we copy and where we beat them

**Copy (table stakes, the leaders all do these)**

- A big "Wyślij zdjęcie na WhatsApp" button above the fold, plus phone and a short form
- Star rating and review count in the hero and in the Google page title, once you have reviews
- "Od" prices in złoty **brutto** (gross) on the homepage
- Three-step process: send a photo, get price and slot, we clean at your place
- A guarantee and OC insurance stated plainly, as Posejdona does
- A stain first-aid guide and a 10% referral reward, as OtoCzystaTapicerka does

**Beat them (no one does these well)**

| Gap | Our move |
| --- | --- |
| No dedicated stroller and car seat page anywhere in Poznań | Make it our flagship page, with brand-specific car seat know-how |
| Pets are an afterthought | A dedicated pet-accident page: urine, hair, smell, fast slots |
| Suburbs pay extra or are just a list | Free travel in Zone 1, zones shown on a map, town pages for Swarzędz, Suchy Las, Luboń, Komorniki |
| Drying times vague or long (up to 36 h) | State it per item, up front: sofa 4–8 h, stroller same day |
| Net prices and 250 zł minimums | Gross prices only, 150 zł minimum, family packages |
| Generic, keyword-stuffed copy | Speak to the four personas in plain, warm Polish |
| No punctuality promise (the only bad review we found was a no-show) | "Jesteśmy punktualnie albo dzwonimy wcześniej" |

**Price check before launch**

Czysta Tapicerka lists strollers, carriers and car seats at 60–120 zł. **Settled:** our prices moved into that range for launch (single stroller 90 zł, 2-in-1 120 zł, car seat 80 zł), with the whole set, frame and wheels included and same-day use as the visible extras. Prices move back toward premium after 30–50 reviews.
