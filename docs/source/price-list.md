# Mobile Cleaning Price List – Poznań

Oct 19, 2026 · @Yari

## How prices are set

Prices sit at or just below the Poznań market average, so you compete on convenience and specialisation rather than undercutting. All prices are in zł, final to the customer, and include travel within Zone 1 (Poznań and the bordering suburbs).

- **Minimum order:** 150 zł per visit, so a single small item never costs you a trip.
- **Travel:** priced by zone, not per km, so suburban families (your core market) pay little or nothing extra. See "Service area" below.
- **"Market" in the Basis column** = based on published Poznań or national price data (sources at the end). **"Estimate"** = no reliable local data found; set by comparison with similar items.

## Service area and travel

Poznań county is the most populous county in Poland and is where most young families live, so the suburbs are treated as home ground.

| Zone | Areas | Travel fee |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Zone 1 | Poznań, Suchy Las, Swarzędz, Luboń, Komorniki, Plewiska, Skórzewo, Tarnowo Podgórne, Rokietnica, Czerwonak | Free |
| Zone 2 | Kórnik, Mosina, Murowana Goślina, Pobiedziska, Buk, Stęszew, Kleszczewo and the rest of Poznań county | 25 zł, free for orders over 400 zł |
| Zone 3 | Beyond Poznań county | 1.50 zł/km from the county border, or by quote |

The "free over 400 zł" rule steers Zone 2 customers towards packages, which makes the longer drive worth it.

| Evening or weekend slot | +15% |

- Ask for photos by WhatsApp or OLX chat before quoting anything heavily soiled.
- Tell customers upholstery needs 4–8 hours to dry; strollers and car seats are usable the same day.
- Some stains (old ink, bleach marks, mould) may not come out fully; say so before starting.
