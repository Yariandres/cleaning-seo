# Customer Questions – Mobile Cleaning, Poznań

Nov 5, 2026 · @Yari

## Key findings

Poznań customers ask about five things, in this order: price, drying time, whether it is worth it versus doing it themselves, whether it is safe for their fabric and family, and how fast you can come.

| Price | "Ile kosztuje…", "cennik", "cena" in nearly every Google suggestion for every service | Strongest |

| Safety and damage | Fabric type, chemicals, kids and allergies, insurance | Medium |

Baby equipment is a clear gap: Google searches for stroller cleaning in Poznań return Warsaw specialists, not a local one.

Scope: online questions rarely state a district, so this covers Poznań-wide Google results plus Polish forums and guides. Facebook neighbourhood groups were sampled; private parents' groups were not.

## What Poznań reviews praise and complain about

Of 389 Google reviews of three Poznań-area cleaners, only one was negative: a cleaner who never turned up. Customers judge on speed, punctuality and visible results, and the bar is a near-perfect rating.

| Pan Jaga (Kościan–Leszno, serves Poznań) | 159 | 0 | Strollers and car seats, pets, price |
| ZAWSZECZYSTO.pl (Bukowska) | 35 | 1 (no-show) | Quick booking, fair prices, free tips |

In Pan Jaga's 159 reviews, pets come up in 37 (23%), speed in 41, price in 33 and children or strollers in 25.

**What customers praise**

- Same-day or next-day visits ("called in the morning, done by the afternoon"; a sofa cleaned just before Christmas)
- Pet accidents fixed: a puppy's urine, a cat using the sofa as a litter tray, cat hair everywhere
- Old stains and furniture they had written off
- Punctual, polite and tidy, and explains the work
- Free advice on handling future stains, such as when the cat has an accident
- A pleasant scent that lasts
- A phone quote before the visit
- Beating a previous firm that "was fast but the result was weak"

**Stroller and car seat stories (Pan Jaga)**

- Cleaning the whole set (carrycot, seat unit, car seat, frame) before a second baby arrives
- A toddler sick in the car seat on the drive back from the seaside, with food even in the base
- A carrycot yellowed by baby oil; a white eco-leather lining that didn't come out, and the owner feared water marks
- A pushchair smeared with sunscreen
- Upsell: a family dropped off a stroller and booked a sofa clean at home when collecting it

**What frustrates them**

- A cleaner not arriving at the agreed time after the customer kept the day free (the only 1-star review)
- Waiting nearly a month for a slot at a busy firm
- Rugs taken away for about 8 working days
- Buyer guides warn about over-wetting, which leaves water marks or mould

## What Facebook groups show

Neighbourhood groups are where jobs get decided: a request draws a flood of replies within hours, and neighbours name the same two or three local cleaners again and again.

- **A real request (Nieformalna Grupa Piątkowska):** a new tenant asked for someone to refresh a 140×200 double mattress after the previous tenants. No stains, no impregnation needed. She doesn't own a Kärcher and wants it done "nie za miliony" (not for a fortune). The post got 23 comments.
- **Who won the replies:** mostly neighbours recommending the same two local cleaners (Pan Gąbka and Marek Pluta), plus a few cleaners pitching themselves. Personal recommendations clearly beat ads.
- **Competitors are everywhere:** cleaners advertise in nearly every district group (Piątkowo, Gowarzewo, Rataje, Junikowo, Mosina). Their usual pitch is "send 2–3 photos for a quote", legal invoices, years of experience and before-and-after photos.
- **A segment to target:** people moving into rented flats, and landlords between tenants, want a quick hygiene refresh rather than stain removal.
- **Not yet checked:** the main parents' groups (Poznańskie Mamy doradzają, MAMY z Poznania, Jeżyckie Mamy Poznań, Mamy z Poznania – Grunwald) are private and return nothing until you join.

## How to use this

Answer the top questions before customers ask them: on your Google Business Profile, OLX listings and a short FAQ.

1. **Put prices in every listing.** Price dominates searches; a visible "od 150 zł" beats "wycena indywidualna".
2. **State drying times plainly.** For example: sofa 4–8 h, car 4–12 h (longer in winter), stroller same day.
3. **Lead with speed.** "Termin nawet tego samego dnia" answers the month-long wait competitors leave open.
4. **Answer the DIY objection.** Show what rented machines leave behind, and promise no over-wetting.
5. **Own baby equipment in Poznań.** Brand-specific car seat know-how, baby-safe products, frame and wheels included.
6. **Ask for reviews that mention these points.** Speed, smell removal and before-and-after photos are what readers trust.

Two more from the full review lists: offer a pet-accident package (urine, hair, odour), since pets come up in about a quarter of reviews; and sell reliability, since the only bad review was a no-show.
