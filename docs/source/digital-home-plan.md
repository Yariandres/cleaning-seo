# Digital Home – Website Plan

Nov 6, 2026 · @Yari

The website is built in five layers, each resting on the one before: **1 Offer → 2 Copy → 3 Content → 4 Landing Pages → 5 Funnel.** This doc starts with Layer 1.

## Layer 1 · Offer

**The offer in one line:** *We clean what your family lives on (sofa, mattress, stroller, car seat and car) at your door in Poznań and the suburbs, at the price quoted from your photos, and usable the same day.*

The three parts work in order:

1. **The menu** says what we sell, in the customer's words, not ours. One item is always highlighted as "wanted now".
2. **The urgency gateway** gives a real reason to book this week rather than "some day".
3. **The promise** is the aspirin: it removes the specific headache that made them search in the first place.

Everything below comes from the research: Google searches, the Piątkowo Facebook request, 389 competitor reviews and the competitor audit.

**The master promise (on every page)**

> **Przyjeżdżamy na czas. Płacisz tyle, ile podaliśmy ze zdjęć. Jeśli coś wyjdzie po wyschnięciu, wracamy za darmo.**

*English: We arrive on time. You pay what we quoted from your photos. If anything shows after drying, we come back free.*

This targets the three pains in the reviews and audit: the only 1-star review was a no-show; competitors' net-plus-VAT pricing creates surprises; only one competitor offers a re-clean guarantee.

**Guarantee terms (so it's a promise we can keep)**

- **On time:** if we're more than 15 minutes late, we call before the agreed time. A no-show gets 50% off the rescheduled visit.
- **Price:** the price quoted from photos is final unless the item clearly differs from the photos. If so, we tell you before starting, and you can say no at no cost.
- **Re-clean:** report within 7 days of the visit, with a photo, and we return free for stains that should have come out.
- **Honest limits:** old ink, bleach marks and mould staining may not come out fully. We say so before starting, and those are excluded from the re-clean.

## Booking assistant (parked for Layer 5 · Funnel)

An assistant on the site and on WhatsApp that answers, quotes and books while you're on a job or off for the evening, then hands you the full job. It also strengthens the offer: "odpowiadamy w kilka minut, także wieczorem" (we reply within minutes, evenings too).

**What it does, end to end**

1. **Answers questions** from our FAQ, price list and guarantee terms only.
2. **Collects the job:** what to clean, 2–3 photos, address, and which zone it falls in, with the travel fee.
3. **Quotes:** gives the price-list price for standard items. For anything unusual (mould, leather, heavy soiling, very large jobs) it says "wycena wstępna" (provisional) and flags it for you.
4. **Books:** offers real free slots from your calendar and reserves one.
5. **Hands over:** sends you a summary (items, photos, address, price, slot, customer phone) by WhatsApp or email. You confirm, change or decline.

**Rules that keep the promise honest**

- Only you confirm a final price for flagged jobs, so "you pay what we quoted" stays true.
- It never invents prices or promises outside the price list and the guarantee.
- The customer can always ask for you; it passes the conversation on and says when you'll reply.

We'll design it properly in Layer 5, once the copy and pages are settled.

Once these are settled, Layer 2 turns this offer into headlines, buttons and page text.

## Layer 2 · Copy

The copy has one job: take the thought already in the customer's head and answer it with our offer, in their words. Every line on the site is either a question they're asking or the answer to one.

**Voice:** a friendly, competent neighbour. Plain Polish, short sentences, no cleaning jargon. We address the customer as **"Ty"** (informal), like OtoCzystaTapicerka, rather than the stiff "Państwo" PranieNaZawolanie24 uses. Parents, pet owners and young tenants respond better to a warm tone.

**Source of the words:** wherever possible, the copy reuses phrases from real Google searches, the Piątkowo Facebook request and the 389 reviews. If customers say "kanapa jak nowa" (sofa like new), we say it too.

### 1. Communicate the value

Value is the result they get, compared with what it would cost them otherwise. We say both.

**The value line (top of the homepage)**

> **Kanapa, wózek i auto czyste u Ciebie w domu – na czas, w cenie ze zdjęć, gotowe tego samego dnia.**

*English: Sofa, stroller and car clean at your home: on time, at the price quoted from your photos, ready the same day.*

It carries four value points in one sentence: the result (czyste), the convenience (u Ciebie w domu), the certainty (na czas, w cenie ze zdjęć) and the speed (tego samego dnia).

**The price of the alternatives (makes our price look small)**

| What they're tempted to do | What it really costs | Our price |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Buy a new sofa | 2,000–4,000 zł and a delivery wait | 3-seat sofa 180 zł |
| Buy a new stroller set | 2,000–3,000 zł | 2-in-1 stroller 120 zł |
| Rent a Kärcher and do it yourself | 89 zł a day, your Saturday, and the risk of water marks | Done for you, often from 150 zł |
| Take the car to a garage | A lost day without the car | Full upholstery 260 zł, at your door |

**Copy line to use:** *"Zanim kupisz nową kanapę albo wózek – wypierz stary za ułamek ceny."* (Before you buy a new sofa or stroller, clean the old one for a fraction of the price.)

## Layer 3 · Content

Content is the foot traffic: the paths that bring people to the digital home and through the door. The door is always the same: **send 2–3 photos for a quote**.

Not every path carries the same traffic. Ranked by what the research showed actually wins jobs in Poznań:

1. **Google** (search, Maps, the Google Business Profile): where people look the moment they need a cleaner. All five leading competitors are found here.
2. **Neighbourhood Facebook groups:** where jobs get decided by recommendation. Pan Gąbka wins jobs with no website at all.
3. **OLX:** 80+ Poznań upholstery listings; customers browse it like a local directory.
4. **Instagram and TikTok:** before-and-after cleaning videos are a proven "satisfying" format, and mobile detailers in Poznań already use them.
5. **AI assistants** (Google's AI overview, ChatGPT, Gemini): Google already shows an AI answer for "pranie kanapy Poznań" with price ranges, citing competitors' sites.
6. **YouTube:** slower to build, but how-to videos keep bringing searches for years.

**Four content themes, used on every channel:**

- **Przed i po** (before and after): the proof.
- **Porady dla rodziców** (tips for parents): car seat safety, stroller care. Builds the stroller niche.
- **Pierwsza pomoc dla tapicerki** (first aid for upholstery): what to do in the first 10 minutes after wine, coffee or a pet accident.
- **Za kulisami** (behind the scenes): a day on the road around Poznań and the suburbs. Puts a face to the business.

### One job, five posts

You won't have time to "make content". So every job produces it, in about 3 minutes on site:

1. **Before:** one photo and a 5-second video of the dirty item, from the same angle.
2. **During:** 5 seconds of the extractor pulling dirty water, or the dirty tank.
3. **After:** the same angle as the before shot.
4. **Review:** hand over a small card with a QR code to your Google review page and say: *"Jeśli wszystko się podoba, będę wdzięczny za opinię – to dla mnie bardzo ważne."* (If you're happy, I'd be grateful for a review; it means a lot to me.)

That one job then becomes:

- a before/after photo on the Google profile
- a Reel/TikTok
- a YouTube Short
- a picture for the service or town page
- a monthly Facebook group post, if the group allows

**Privacy rule:** ask permission before filming, and never show faces, house numbers, number plates or anything that identifies where the customer lives. Film the item, not the home.

## Layer 5 · Funnel

Most visitors aren't ready to book today. The funnel gives them a reason to stay in touch, so that when the stroller gets dirty, the baby is due or the car is being sold, we're the first name they think of. Each counter has its own funnel, tagged so we can see what each one brings.

**Four additions that make it work for a local service**

- **Ask for their date.** The strongest opt-in question isn't "what's your email" but "when do you need it?": due date, move-in date, car sale date, Christmas. The reminder then lands exactly when they're ready, which is the "right time" you described.

## Business model: solo first, built to expand

The business runs as **one person, premium, VAT-exempt**. Everything (prices, website, funnel, checklists) is built so that partner cleaners or a new city can plug in later without rebuilding anything.

**The solo target: just under the VAT limit**

From 2026 a business stays VAT-exempt while yearly revenue is under **240,000 zł**. Under that limit the whole price is yours; above it, about 19% of every price goes to VAT. So the target is about **19,500 zł revenue a month (235,000 zł a year)**, which leaves roughly **13,000 zł a month** after materials, fuel, ZUS, health insurance and ryczałt tax. In the first year the limit is reduced in proportion to the days the business is open, so check the exact figure with an accountant before launch.

| Average job | Jobs a month to reach 19,500 zł | Jobs a day (22 working days) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 300 zł | 65 | 3.0 |
| 350 zł | 56 | 2.5 |
| 400 zł | 49 | 2.2 |

**The profit levers, in order of impact**

1. **A higher average job, not more jobs.** Lead with packages, offer add-ons at every visit (fabric protection +30%, anti-allergy +20 zł, ozone, the second side of a mattress), and ask the "while I'm here" question: *"Skoro już jestem – fotelik w aucie za 80 zł?"*
2. **Area days.** Group jobs by area (e.g. Monday Piątkowo and Suchy Las, Tuesday Swarzędz and Rataje). The booking assistant offers only that day's area.
3. **Prices back up once reviews arrive.** After 30–50 reviews, move stroller and car seat prices back toward premium.
4. **Repeat and referral work instead of ads.** The after-job loop and the 12-month reminders.
5. **Business clients for quiet weekdays and winter.** Letting agents, car dealers, nurseries.
6. **Low costs.** Ryczałt 8.5%, preferential ZUS in the first years, free or cheap tool plans, bulk chemicals.

The spreadsheet Equipment\_Budget\_Poznan.xlsx has a "Solo profit" sheet to test these numbers.

### Systems to build now

They save you time as a solo operator, and they're exactly what a partner or a new city would plug into.

- **A checklist per counter:** the same steps, products and drying advice for every stroller, sofa, mattress, car and pet job. Write them while cleaning your own family's items.
- **Photos on every job:** before, during and after, same angles. Quality control, content and protection if a result is disputed.
- **A job card:** the booking assistant records items, photos, address, price, zone and notes for every job.
- **Calendars by person and area:** the booking tool supports more than one calendar from day one, even if only yours is switched on.
- **The guarantee belongs to the brand:** re-clean requests come to the business, not to whoever did the job.
- **Weekly numbers:** jobs, revenue, average job value, re-cleans, reviews, and revenue against the 240,000 zł limit.
- **A brand not tied to Poznań:** the business name (still to research) works in any city. Page addresses carry the city (`/poznan/pranie-wozkow-i-fotelikow`), so `/wroclaw/…` slots in later.

### Expansion 1: autonomous partner cleaners

Partners are cleaners with their own business (JDG). They pay their own tax and ZUS, own or rent their kit, and set their own availability. We supply the brand, the jobs and the system. There are two ways the money can flow, and the choice matters because of the VAT limit.

|  | A. Subcontractor | B. Brand partner (recommended) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Who invoices the customer | You | The partner |
| How you earn | The job price minus what the partner invoices you (e.g. you keep 35%) | A fee per booked job (e.g. 20% of the job value) |
| Counts toward your 240,000 zł limit | The whole job price | Only your fees |
| Effect | You hit the VAT limit quickly | You can stay solo and VAT-exempt for longer |

**Example, one brand partner per month:** 55 jobs × 300 zł = 16,500 zł billed by the partner; a 20% fee = **\~3,300 zł to you**, with almost no extra cost. Three partners ≈ 9,900 zł a month. Your fees and your own cleaning count toward the same 240,000 zł limit, so at that point you'd clean less yourself or register for VAT. The fee level is an example to test.

**What a partner brings:** their own JDG, insurance and vehicle, the equipment kit (bought, or rented from you), and agreement to follow the checklists, the photo rule and the guarantee.

**What we provide:** the brand, the jobs from the website and booking assistant, training, checklists, the price list, and chemicals at bulk prices.

**Keeping it genuinely autonomous:** partners choose their hours, can turn jobs down and may work for other clients. If they work like employees (fixed hours, only for us, under our direction), the arrangement can be reclassified as employment.

**Before the first partner:** register the business name as a trademark (Polish Patent Office, UPRP) so it can be licensed, and have a lawyer draft the partner agreement and an accountant check the fee and VAT setup.

### Cash changes the cash-register picture

Checked 7 Nov 2026 — confirm with the accountant:

- The cash-register exemption we planned on (**100% cashless payments identifying the payer**) is lost as soon as we accept cash.
- Without it, private-customer sales are exempt only up to **20,000 zł a year** (prorated in the first year). Above that, a cash register becomes mandatory — at our targets that is reached within the first months.
- **Virtual (app-only) registers are not allowed for upholstery / cleaning services.** From 1 Apr 2026 they are allowed for car washing (PKWiU 45.20.30) regardless of how it is provided — so the car counter alone could use one, but the main services cannot.
- **Plan:** buy a **mobile online cash register** (kasa online, roughly 1,000–1,500 zł; the register relief can refund up to 700 zł) and record **every private-customer sale, cash and card**, from day one. The customer gets a paragon at the job. One device covers all services, so the separate virtual register for car jobs is no longer needed.
- The app keeps a per-job sales log that matches the register, and the Dziś screen reminds the owner to print the paragon when a job is marked paid.

## 5. Invoices and records

- VAT-exempt (art. 113) sales are documented with a **faktura "zw."**, never a "rachunek". KSeF applies to VAT-exempt businesses; the final stage and penalties start **1 Jan 2027**, before our launch, so everything goes through KSeF from day one.
- **Fakturownia** via API: a faktura for every business customer, and for any private customer who asks (mandatory if asked within 3 months). Paragon from the cash register for private customers by default.
- Exports for the accountant from Fakturownia; the Raport screen tracks revenue against the **240,000 zł VAT limit**.

## 7. Data

- **Customer** identified by phone or email; many addresses; many jobs.
- **Job:** line items from the price list, zone + travel fee, photos, notes, payment method, source (counter + channel + UTM), stage history, linked re-clean jobs.
- **Visit length** = sum of item durations (e.g. single stroller 45 min, 3-seat sofa 90 min) + travel buffer by zone; all editable.
- **Area days** (e.g. Tue Swarzędz / Kórnik, Thu Luboń / Komorniki) order the slots shown; they are not hard rules.
- **Retention:** quote photos 12 months; enquiries that never booked 12 months; customers, jobs and invoices 5 years; marketing consent until withdrawn. Photos used publicly only with separate consent. The privacy policy states exactly this.
