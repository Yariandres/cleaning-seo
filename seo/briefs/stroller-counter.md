## 1. Search intent

**Who searches:** parents in Poznań and the county with a dirty stroller, carrycot or child car seat, often before a second baby, after a sick-in-the-car-seat trip, or with a second-hand set. They want **someone local, a price and to know it's safe for a baby**. Many search the DIY route first ("jak wyczyścić wózek", "jak prać fotelik cybex").

**What Google shows today** (google.com, `hl=pl&gl=pl`, read 8 Nov 2026 from the owner's computer; results can vary by location and person):

*"pranie wózków dziecięcych poznań"*
- **No map pack.** A single business panel for ZAWSZECZYSTO.pl (4.7, 35 reviews), a carpet/upholstery firm.
- Organic, in order: czysto.poznan.pl (Zawsze Czysto drop-off laundry) · zawszeczysto.pl (same firm, second domain) · OLX category · **CleanWhale** Poznań stroller page (title "Pranie wózka dziecięcego Poznań – cena od 99 zł") · **Kubus Serwis** (Facebook, stroller repair + cleaning, 150–250 zł) · marekpluta.pl (page "w trakcie przygotowania") · Lux Clean blog post · naprawawozkow.pl (stroller repair service).
- **Ludzie pytają też:** Ile kosztuje pranie wózka dziecięcego? · Gdzie w Poznaniu można naprawiać wózki dziecięce? · Jak prać gondolę wózka dziecięcego? · Jak odświeżyć kolor wózka dla dzieci?
- No AI Overview in the text read.

*"pranie fotelika samochodowego poznań"*
- **Map pack** of upholstery cleaners: OtoCzystaTapicerka (5.0, 195), Czysta Tapicerka Poznań (5.0, 105), Panjaga (5.0, 159). The extended list includes two baby shops (Osiem Gwiazdek, BoboWózki).
- Organic is mostly **car-upholstery** pages; only the two Zawsze Czysto pages and Marek Pluta are about child seats.
- **Ludzie pytają też:** Ile kosztuje pranie fotelika samochodowego? · Jak prać fotelik samochodowy?

**What that means**
- The ranking pages are weak on what Kasia wants: Zawsze Czysto is **drop-off, about 3 days**, no brands, no drying time for the customer; CleanWhale is a thin template page (~700 words including navigation, an add-on to flat cleaning); Pluta is empty; Kubus is Facebook only. **Nobody offers same-day cleaning at the customer's door with brand-specific car-seat care.** That is the angle, and it's true for us.
- The **price gap is real**: drop-off from 30–35 zł vs our stroller 90 zł / car seat 80 zł. The page has to make the difference visible (at your door, same day, whole set incl. frame and wheels, no trip with a baby). Pricing is the owner's call; flagged, not changed.
- The car-seat query is **won in the map pack**, so Google Business Profile services (stroller, car seat, with prices) matter as much as the page. Goes in the GBP plan.

## 3. Page elements (proposals — final wording in brand voice)

- **Title tag** (≤ 60 chars), pick one:
  - `Pranie wózków i fotelików Poznań – u Ciebie, od 80 zł` (53) — price in the title, like CleanWhale's, but true "od" and a mobile angle
  - `Pranie wózków dziecięcych Poznań – u Ciebie w domu` (50) — closest to the primary query
  - Once real reviews exist, consider adding the count (competitors do this); not before.
- **Meta description** (≤ 155 chars), pick one:
  - `Pierzemy wózki, gondole i foteliki u Ciebie w domu, w Poznaniu i okolicach. Gotowe tego samego dnia. Cena ze zdjęć przed przyjazdem, od 80 zł.` (142)
  - `Wózek, gondola, fotelik i stelaż w jednej wizycie, pod Twoim domem w Poznaniu i okolicach. Gotowe jeszcze dziś. Wyślij 2–3 zdjęcia – podamy cenę.` (145)
- **H1:** the existing hero line ("Wózek, fotelik i kanapa jak nowe – bez wychodzenia z domu") has **no "pranie wózków" and no "Poznań"**, and "kanapa" pulls it toward the sofa page. Two options for the brand strategy:
  - (a) H1 carries the search phrase, hero line stays as the big visual headline: H1 `Pranie wózków i fotelików dziecięcych w Poznaniu i okolicach`, styled smaller as an eyebrow above the headline; or
  - (b) Reword the headline itself, e.g. "Wózek i fotelik jak nowe – pranie u Ciebie w domu, w Poznaniu i okolicach" (drops "kanapa").
  Either works for SEO; (a) keeps the copy untouched.
- **H2 outline in order** (counter anatomy, PRD S2):
  1. *Proof bar* — no H2 needed (short facts: same day, at your door, manufacturer's instructions, free re-clean). No ratings until real.
  2. *Headache:* H2 around "why not wash it yourself" — e.g. "Dlaczego nie wyprać wózka samemu?" (water marks, shrinking, straps must not be soaked)
  3. *What's included + price:* H2 with "cena / cennik", e.g. "Pranie wózka i fotelika – cena" — table of items with gross "od" prices from the price list (single stroller, 2-in-1, 3-in-1, twin, car seat, high chair/bouncer) + the Nowy Członek Rodziny / Rodzinny / Auto i Maluch packages
  4. *Steps:* H2 "Jak to działa" — 3 steps (photos → price and slot → we clean at your door)
  5. *Before/after:* H2 only when real photos exist (PRD S6); hidden otherwise
  6. *Car seats by brand:* H2 e.g. "Foteliki Cybex, Maxi-Cosi, Britax, Avionaut – według instrukcji producenta" — short text on cover, shell and straps (see FAQ). Only brands whose manuals the owner has actually checked (open item in `price-list.md`)
  7. *FAQ:* H2 "Najczęstsze pytania"
  8. *Service area:* H2 e.g. "Gdzie dojeżdżamy" — Poznań + Zone 1 towns as text, link to `/poznan/obszar-dzialania`
- **FAQ questions** (all real, see §2; answers from the owner's facts):
  1. Ile kosztuje pranie wózka dziecięcego? — prices by type, exact price from photos
  2. Ile kosztuje pranie fotelika samochodowego? — 80 zł, in packages cheaper
  3. Jak pierzecie foteliki Cybex czy Maxi-Cosi? — per the manual: cover per its label, shell and buckle wiped with mild detergent, **straps surface-cleaned only, never soaked** (matches Maxi-Cosi's and Cybex's published guidance: harness wiped with a cloth, no harsh chemicals or solvents, covers dried fully before refitting)
  4. Czy środki są bezpieczne dla niemowlęcia? — hypoallergenic products
  5. Kiedy wózek będzie gotowy? — same day (vs drop-off laundries' days; don't name competitors)
  6. Czy usuniecie pleśń z gondoli? / Czy odświeżycie wyblakły kolor? — honest answer, pick one per what the owner can deliver (price list: mould may not fully come out — say so)
  7. *(optional)* Czy czyścicie też stelaż i koła? — yes, included
- **Terms to use naturally:** wózek dziecięcy, spacerówka, gondola, wózek 2w1 / 3w1, wózek bliźniaczy, fotelik samochodowy, nosidełko, pokrowiec, stelaż, koła, pasy, pleśń, plamy po oliwce / kremie z filtrem, hipoalergiczne środki, tego samego dnia, z dojazdem, u Ciebie w domu; brands (Cybex, Maxi-Cosi, Britax Römer, Avionaut, Joie, Venicci) only where true; towns: Poznań, Swarzędz, Suchy Las, Luboń, Komorniki, Plewiska, Tarnowo Podgórne (in the area section, not stuffed in copy).

## 5. Structured data

Rendered server-side (validate the rendered HTML in the audit, not the source):
- **Service** — `serviceType: "Pranie wózków dziecięcych i fotelików samochodowych"`, `provider` → the LocalBusiness (service-area business: no street address, `areaServed` = Poznań + Zone 1 and Zone 2 towns from the towns list), `offers` → one **Offer** per item with `priceSpecification` (`priceCurrency: "PLN"`, `minPrice` from `{price:…}`), so the "od" prices stay honest.
- **FAQPage** from `{faq:…}`, same text as on the page. Note: Google shows FAQ rich results only for a few authoritative sites now, so expect no stars-and-dropdowns in results; the markup still helps AI answers.
- **BreadcrumbList:** Strona główna → Pranie wózków i fotelików.
- **No AggregateRating / Review** until real Google reviews exist and are entered by the owner.

## 6. Images

- File names in Polish, hyphenated, describing the content: `pranie-gondoli-wozka-przed-po.webp`, `pranie-fotelika-samochodowego-poznan.webp`.
- Alt text describes what's in the photo, plain Polish: "Gondola wózka przed i po praniu – plama po oliwce usunięta". No keyword lists.
- Only **real job photos** with the customer's consent (PRD S6); stock photos get empty-ish decorative alt and aren't presented as our work.
- The hero image should not carry the H1 or prices as text inside the image.
- WebP/AVIF, explicit width/height, lazy-load below the fold (Core Web Vitals target in PRD §6).

## 7. Notes for the designer

- **Gross "od" prices visible high up** (at least the stroller and car seat price in or right below the hero) — price is the #1 question and competitors' result titles already show prices.
- **Same day + at your door** must be readable in the first screen; it's the one thing the ranking competitors (drop-off, 3 days) can't say.
- FAQ as **real HTML text**, answers visible or in native `<details>`; not images, not loaded only after a click via script.
- H1 as text, one per page; the H2 order above can follow the counter anatomy exactly.
- Area section as text (town names), map optional on top.
- Leave room for a review strip that stays **hidden until real reviews exist**.

## 8. Success measure

Check in Search Console 4–8 weeks after launch:
- The page is indexed on the brand domain; staging never appears.
- Impressions for the primary and secondary queries; average position for "pranie wózków dziecięcych poznań" and "pranie wózka dziecięcego poznań" (target: page 1, then top 3 — a target, not a forecast).
- Click-through rate on the title (if low with impressions, test the other title option).
- Google Business Profile: the profile appears in the map pack for "pranie fotelika samochodowego poznań"; calls and website clicks.
- From the app's source report: enquiries tagged to this counter from organic Google.
