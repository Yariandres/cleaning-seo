# SEO strategy (starting point)

Status: **updated 10 Nov 2026** after keyword validation (`seo/keywords/keyword-map.md`), approved by the owner. The SEO agent refines it with real research; the owner approves changes.

## Where we start

From `docs/source/competitor-audit.md` and `customer-questions.md`:

- **The #1 spot is beatable.** PranieNaZawolanie24 ranks #1 for "pranie kanapy Poznań" and "pranie tapicerki Poznań" with an old, text-heavy page — no prices on the homepage, no reviews, no WhatsApp.
- **Stroller pages exist, but they're weak** (Google, 8 Nov 2026): drop-off laundries (Zawsze Czysto, from 30–35 zł, about 3 days), a booking platform (CleanWhale, "od 99 zł"), a stroller repair service on Facebook (Kubus Serwis) and an empty page (Marek Pluta). **Nobody offers same-day cleaning at your door with brand-specific car-seat care.** Car-seat searches are won in the map pack by upholstery cleaners.
- **Nobody owns pets.** No local competitor has a dedicated pet page.
- **Suburbs are under-served.** Competitors charge extra or just list town names.
- **Word of mouth ranks too.** Pan Gąbka has no website, only Facebook, and still ranks — reviews and Google Business Profile carry real weight.

## Priorities

1. **Win the weakly held niches first:** stroller and car seat cleaning in Poznań (flagship counter) on the at-your-door, same-day angle, then pet accidents.
2. **Compete on the core money terms** with clearly better pages: sofa/upholstery, mattress.
3. **Google Business Profile + reviews** from day one — the map pack is where local clicks go.
4. **Suburbs** through the service-area page and GBP service areas; town pages only once there's real local proof.
5. **AI answers:** clear facts (area, prices, drying times, safety) in FAQ form so AI Overviews and chat assistants can cite us.
